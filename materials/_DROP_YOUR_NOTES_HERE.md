# drop your stuff here

This folder is where bart looks for your course materials. Drop in any combination of:

- **PDFs** — textbook chapters, past exams, homework, lecture handouts
- **PowerPoint** (`.pptx`) — lecture slides
- **Word docs** (`.docx`) — assignments, study guides
- **Markdown** (`.md`) — your own notes
- **Plain text** (`.txt`) — anything else

You can drop them flat in this folder, or organize them in subfolders — bart will find them all.

When you're ready, run `./run` from the parent directory.

**You can delete this file once you've added your materials.** It's just here to say hi.
