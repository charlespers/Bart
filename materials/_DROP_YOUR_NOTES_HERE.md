# drop your course materials here

This folder is where bart looks for your course materials. Supported types:

- **PDFs** — textbook chapters, past exams, homework, lecture handouts
- **PowerPoint** (`.pptx`) — lecture slides
- **Word docs** (`.docx`) — assignments, study guides
- **Markdown** (`.md`) — plain notes
- **Plain text** (`.txt`) — anything else

Files can be flat in this folder or organized in subfolders — bart will find them all.

When ready, run `./run` from the parent directory.

This placeholder file can be deleted once you've added your materials.
